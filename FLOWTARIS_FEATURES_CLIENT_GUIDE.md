# Flowtaris AI Platform — Feature Guide for Clients

## Overview

Flowtaris AI (flowtaris.ai) is the enterprise AI automation platform developed by Flowtaris, designed specifically for finance teams operating on major ERP systems (NetSuite, Coupa, SAP, Workday). This platform serves as the **innovation flagship** in the Flowtaris digital ecosystem, demonstrating the company's AI and automation capabilities through interactive, proof-of-concept experiences.

While flowtaris.com represents the core Flowtaris business (services, industries, company information), flowtaris.ai focuses on **what we've built and where we're going** — showcasing live proof of AI capabilities that enterprise buyers can interact with, calculate ROI from, and request demos for.

## Core Features Explained

### 🚀 1. The Flowtaris Intelligence Suite — Four Interactive Tools

The homepage features four real-time, interactive calculators that allow prospects to experience AI value immediately:

#### **AI Readiness Assessment**
- **What it does:** 6-question diagnostic that scores your ERP environment across Quick Wins, Strategic plays, and Innovation bets
- **Why we need it:** Enterprise buyers distrust vague AI promises. This tool provides a personalized roadmap in <4 minutes, turning abstract "AI potential" into concrete, prioritized actions
- **Key features:**
  - Dynamic wizard with real-time feedback
  - Scoring algorithm that categorizes opportunities
  - Email delivery of personalized roadmap
  - No signup required — instant value delivery

#### **Enterprise ROI Calculator**
- **What it does:** Drag sliders to input invoice volume, team size, and error rate; see real-time savings projections across 15 currencies and 5 ERP platforms
- **Why we need it:** Finance leaders need board-ready numbers, not marketing claims. This tool translates AI capabilities into hard financial metrics
- **Key features:**
  - Live animation as sliders move
  - Multi-currency, multi-ERP support
  - Projects 3-year NPV and payback period
  - One-click CFO report generation

#### **Cost of Inaction Engine**
- **What it does:** Live counter showing monthly revenue leakage from delayed AI adoption, plus compliance risk and competitive gap calculations
- **Why we need it:** Creates urgency by quantifying the cost of delay — critical for overcoming status quo bias in enterprise sales
- **Key features:**
  - Real-time accumulating counter since page load
  - Board-ready PDF export with 3-year modeling
  - Visualizes impact of 6-month delays
  - Compliance risk quantification

#### **Innovation Lab Explorer**
- **What it does:** Interactive peek into Flowtaris's active R&D tracks with maturity indicators and beta access requests
- **Why we need it:** Positions Flowtaris as a technology leader, not just a vendor. Shows continuous innovation beyond current offerings
- **Key features:**
  - Six active research tracks (GenAI Document Understanding, Conversational ERP, etc.)
  - Maturity indicators (Research → Pilot → Production)
  - Beta program access requests
  - Technology readiness scoring

### 💼 2. Capabilities Showcase — Six Production AI Modules

Beyond calculators, the platform demonstrates six specific AI capabilities solving expensive finance problems:

#### **GenAI Document Intelligence**
- **Problem:** Finance teams lose 23 hours/week manually keying documents
- **Solution:** AI model extracts, classifies, validates fields across 140+ document types
- **Proof:** 99.4% extraction accuracy across 2.1M documents processed
- **Why it matters:** Eliminates manual data entry — a universal pain point in finance operations

#### **Autonomous Workflow Engine**
- **Problem:** Exception queues grow faster than teams can clear them
- **Solution:** Maps approval paths, learns from past decisions, auto-resolves exceptions
- **Proof:** 91% of exceptions auto-resolved without human touch
- **Why it matters:** Reduces processing delays from days to minutes, improves cash flow

#### **Predictive Analytics**
- **Problem:** Cash flow surprises kill quarter-ends; ERP shows gaps too late
- **Solution:** Rolling forecast models on historical transactions + external signals
- **Proof:** 88% forecast accuracy with 45-day early warning window
- **Why it matters:** Transforms finance from reactive reporting to proactive planning

#### **Conversational ERP Interface**
- **Problem:** ERP requires certification to run simple reports
- **Solution:** Natural language → ERP query translation, no training required
- **Proof:** 74% reduction in ERP-related support tickets in 60 days
- **Why it matters:** Democratizes ERP access — power users aren't the only ones who can get insights

#### **Integration Health Monitoring**
- **Problem:** Data sync failures between systems go undetected for hours
- **Solution:** Instruments every API call/pipeline with real-time health probes + auto-healing
- **Proof:** Reduced MTTR from 52 minutes to 4 minutes across 340+ integrations
- **Why it matters:** Prevents data corruption and reporting errors that undermine trust in finance systems

#### **AI Governance & Compliance**
- **Problem:** Auditors ask "How did the AI make this decision?" — most AI platforms can't answer
- **Solution:** Immutable, human-readable audit records for every AI action
- **Proof:** 100% decision traceability, SOC 2 Type II architecture
- **Why it matters:** Enables auditability and regulatory compliance — essential for enterprise adoption

## Implementation & Technical Notes

### Built With
- **Framework:** Next.js 14 (App Router) with React 18
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS with custom design system
- **CMS:** Sanity.io (content managed by Flowtaris team)
- **Deployment:** Vercel (edge-optimized, ISR enabled)
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics 4 + custom event tracking
- **Type Safety:** TypeScript throughout

### Key Technical Decisions
1. **Interactive over Static:** Every major feature has a live, interactive component rather than just descriptions
2. **Progressive Disclosure:** Complex information revealed through interaction (accordions, wizards, sliders)
3. **Mobile-First with Graceful Degradation:** Full epic experience on desktop, optimized essentials on mobile
4. **Accessibility First:** All interactions keyboard-navigable and screen-reader friendly
5. **Performance Budget:** Strict limits on animated elements to maintain 60fps

### Content Management Model
All content is client-editable through Sanity Studio:
- **AI Capabilities:** Technical details, metrics, demo videos
- **Case Studies:** Client info, challenge/solution/results, testimonials
- **Insight Articles:** Technical blog posts for SEO/GEO
- **Platform Pages:** ERP-specific capabilities and integrations
- **ROI Calculator:** Assumptions, formulas, benchmark data
- **Team Bios:** Leadership, practice leads, partner information
